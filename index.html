FAQ

Common questions

Can I use my own phone number with the AI Call Agent?
Yes! You can port your existing number to VoxAssist, or we can provision a new number (including 03059586156) that connects instantly to the AI Call Agent. The setup typically takes under 24 hours and requires no technical expertise on your end.
What's the difference between the ChatBot and the Call Agent?
The AI ChatBot handles text-based conversations — on your website, WhatsApp, or Facebook Messenger. It's ideal for lead capture, FAQs, and support. The AI Call Agent handles voice phone calls, both inbound and outbound, with a natural human-like voice. Both can be used together for full coverage across all channels.
How long does it take to set up VoxAssist?
Most customers are live within 24–48 hours. Our team handles the entire onboarding — from training the AI on your business data to deploying on your preferred channels. No coding skills required. Simply share your FAQs, product info, and preferences and we handle the rest.
Is my data secure with VoxAssist?
Absolutely. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest using AES-256 encryption. We are GDPR-compliant and never share or sell your data to third parties. Enterprise customers get dedicated data isolation for maximum security.
